## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the aura of a high-end cultural institution and contemporary art gallery. It strips away standard transactional commerce patterns in favor of calm, deliberate curation inspired by luxury independent periodicals and haute atelier portfolios.

### Key Tenets
- **Quiet Authority:** Premium experience is asserted through expansive negative space, deliberate typography, and restraint rather than loud ornamentation.
- **Editorial Archival Tone:** Every item is presented as an artwork with provenance, material narrative, and maker context.
- **Visual Tension:** Deliberate asymmetry contrasts sweeping serif headings with utilitarian, hyper-legible sans-serif specifications.
- **Tactile Materiality:** Background tones emulate heavyweight uncoated cotton paper, while borders function as physical hairline deckle cuts.

## Colors

The palette reproduces physical material surfaces found in print printmaking and ceramic studios:

- **Canvas & Surfaces:** Primary background is `#FBF9F5` (Warm Ivory), with secondary layer panels and drawers set in `#F4EFEA` (Soft Cream).
- **Text & Core Hierarchy:** Dominant display text, primary actions, and hero headers utilize `#1C1815` (Deep Espresso). Secondary editorial body copy, metadata, and makers' attributions utilize `#2B2623` (Rich Charcoal). Muted metadata and tertiary notes sit in `#7A7269`.
- **Accents:** `#C86D51` (Muted Terracotta) serves as the primary focal accent for state indicators, curated editorial callouts, and bespoke badges. `#B89758` (Antique Burnished Gold) acts as an elevated metallic accent for limited editions, certifications, and subtle highlights.
- **Structure & Hairlines:** Structural divides, grid scaffolding, and component boundaries use `#E8E2D8` (Sand Hairline).

## Typography

The type system creates an intentional dialogue between traditional editorial craftsmanship and contemporary digital precision.

- **Headlines (Playfair Display):** Set with relaxed, generous line-heights and slight tracking reductions to replicate high-fashion print titles. Used exclusively for artwork titles, narrative storytelling, and marquee introductions. Italicized cuts are reserved for maker quotes, provenance notes, and volume identifiers.
- **Body & Functional (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Provides an objective, highly legible contrast for artwork specifications, dimensions, material compositions, pricing, and transactional mechanisms.
- **Label & Micro-Metadata (Plus Jakarta Sans Caps):** Rendered in uppercase with `0.12em` tracking for category flags, edition numbers, provenance stamps, and curation labels.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is grounded in a 12-column architectural fluid framework designed for deliberate asymmetry and pacing.

### Layout Mechanics
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column structure with `margin` of `4rem` (expanding to max-width container bounds of 1680px) and `gutter` of `2rem`. Asymmetrical compositions are encouraged: 7-column hero visuals offset by 5-column editorial text blocks, or alternating 8/4 rhythm for long-form artisan essays.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8-column layout with `2rem` margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Product pairs feature alternating vertical offsets to preserve the lookbook cadence.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `1.25rem` margin and `1rem` gutter. Visual density remains uncrowded: full-width bleed imagery framed by thin internal borders.
- **Rhythm:** Spacing between disparate editorial sections relies on `space-xl` (`6rem`), creating quiet visual pauses between collections. Component interiors adhere to strict combinations of `space-sm` (`0.75rem`) and `space-md` (`1.5rem`).

## Elevation & Depth

This design system rejects synthetic drop shadows, skeuomorphic blurs, and floating cards. Depth is purely architectural, physical, and planar.

- **Hairline Delimitation:** Surfaces are partitioned solely using `1px solid #E8E2D8`. Grid cells, headers, and footer blocks meet edge-to-edge as joined structural panels.
- **Tonal Stepping:** Layering is achieved via adjacent tonal planes. Floating or modal elements (e.g., cart slide-outs, curation filters) render in `#F4EFEA` or solid `#1C1815`, cleanly sliding above `#FBF9F5` base canvases without drop shadows.
- **Backdrop Overlays:** Modal curtains utilize deep espresso at 40% opacity (`rgba(28, 24, 21, 0.40)`) with zero backdrop blur, maintaining razor-sharp graphic clarity.
- **Depth via Image Framing:** Artwork photography rests inside subtle `#E8E2D8` recessed frames or full-bleed containers, prioritizing the work's inherent light and dimension.

## Shapes

The design system implements a pure `0px` border-radius rule across all elements.

- **Sharp Edges:** Every element—including buttons, input fields, image viewports, tags, dropdown menus, and popovers—has crisp, 90-degree corners.
- **Rationale:** Strict geometric corners reflect the unyielding lines of architectural gallery plinths, framed canvases, and hand-bound editorial leaves.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#1C1815`, text `#FBF9F5`, 0px border radius, zero shadow. Sizing uses `space-md` horizontal padding, `14px` vertical padding. Typography uses `label-caps` in uppercase. On hover, background shifts smoothly to `#C86D51`.
- **Secondary / Outline:** Background transparent, `1px solid #1C1815`, text `#1C1815`. On hover, background fills with `#1C1815` and text reverses to `#FBF9F5`.
- **Ghost Editorial:** Text `#1C1815` with an animated `1px` underline positioned `4px` beneath text. On hover, underline expands from 0 to 100% width or transitions to `#C86D51`.

### Form Fields & Inputs
- **Text Inputs:** Top, left, and right borders are omitted; only a single `1px solid #E8E2D8` bottom baseline border is used. Background is transparent. Placeholder text is styled in `#7A7269` with `body-sm`. Focused state shifts the baseline border to `#1C1815` without focus rings or glows.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Sharp square boxes (`16x16px`) with `1px solid #2B2623` borders and transparent interiors. Checked state displays a solid inner `#1C1815` square inset by `3px`. Radio elements maintain square form factors with an inner `#1C1815` mark to maintain architectural consistency.

### Chips & Badges
- **Curatorial Badges:** Rectangular, sharp tags. `1px solid #E8E2D8` border, `#F4EFEA` background, text `#2B2623`. Styled using `label-caps`.
- **Status / Edition Markers:** Rare items or limited editions use `#FBF9F5` background, `1px solid #B89758`, and `#9B7E46` typography.

### Cards & Artifact Display
- **Artisan Product Card:** Seamless container without background fill or drop shadows. Composed of an image frame (`aspect-ratio: 4/5` or `1/1`), hairline bottom border separator (`#E8E2D8`), followed by category marker in `label-caps`, artifact title in `title-editorial`, and price formatted via `price-numeral`.
- **Editorial Banner Card:** Asymmetric two-column split panel. Left column displays full-bleed artisan process photography; right column (`#F4EFEA`) contains narrative text set in `headline-md` and `body-md` with author provenance.

### Lists & Navigation
- **Navigation Bar:** Fixed top bar, `1px solid #E8E2D8` bottom border, warm ivory `#FBF9F5` fill. Minimal brand signature paired with uppercase tracked menu items.
- **Provenance Accordions:** Clean horizontal list items separated by top-and-bottom hairline dividers. Header features Playfair Display title with a minimal `+` / `—` glyph indicator. Content expands downward revealing sans-serif archival specifications.
