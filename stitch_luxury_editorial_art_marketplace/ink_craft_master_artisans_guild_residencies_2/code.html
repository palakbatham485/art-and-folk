Kyoto Pottery Guild • 4th Generation Master

Mei Lin Takahashi

Practice: Anagama Kiln 96-Hour Natural Ash Glaze & Volcanic Shigaraki Clay

“The clay does not bend to human impatience; it enters an ancient pact with mountain fire and pine ash. To rush the kiln is to silence the stone.”

Takahashi works in complete isolation on the cedar-slopes above Lake Biwa. Each unglazed piece undergoes a continuous four-day firing in her historic split-level wood kiln, reaching 1,320°C.

No synthetic fluxes or chemical pigments are admitted; colors arise solely from molten fly-ash drifting through chamber drafts, producing unpredictable flashes of burnt amber, vitrified moss, and carbon trapping.
