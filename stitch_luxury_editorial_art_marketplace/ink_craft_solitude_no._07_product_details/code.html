Curatorial Essay

The Weight of Stillness in Modern Expression

"A meditation on empty spaces, painted during the fourth week of cold winter rain in Vance's Yanaka atelier."

In Solitude No. 07, Helena Vance strips compositional balance down to fundamental emotional gravity. Created during her celebrated 2024 residency in Tokyo's historic Yanaka district, Vance dialogues directly with the Japanese aesthetic of Ma (negative space) while maintaining the vigorous, raw energy of post-war action abstraction.

The physical ground of the canvas is prepared through seven successive coats of warm animal-glue gesso, textured by hand with coarse pumice before any ink touches the weave. Vance then pours pure Kyoto pine-soot sumi ink directly onto the inclined linen, guiding its descent with rhythmic sweeps of a broad Japanese goat-hair brush.

"True silence is not an absence of sound; it is the physical density of presence. This canvas was created to serve as an anchor in a distracted room."

— Helena Vance, Tokyo Studio Journal, October 2024

Master Artisan Spotlight

Helena Vance

Born 1986, Edinburgh · Based in London & Tokyo

Helena Vance’s studio practice occupies the intersection between European tactile material painting and Far Eastern calligraphic meditation. A graduate of the Royal College of Art, her solo exhibitions have been mounted at Kyoto Municipal Museum of Art and the Whitechapel Gallery. Vance produces no more than twelve masterworks each year.
