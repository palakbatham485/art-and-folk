CONSTITUTION OF THE LIVING WORKSHOP

The Four Pillars of the Handmade Charter

Every guild affiliated with INK & CRAFT adheres to non-negotiable standards of environmental extraction, generational remuneration, and physical authenticity.

01

Slow Materiality

Strict prohibition of petroleum-derived polymers and synthetic dyes. Only organic fibers, botanical colorants, naturally fallen mountain timber, and geologically sourced clays may be utilized.

eco 100% Biodegradable Base
02

Physical NFC Ledger

Every work features an invisible, kiln-fired or resin-sealed micro-ceramic NFC tag. Tapping the object reveals its chronological kiln firing logs, workshop coordinates, and signed provenance certificate.

nfc Unalterable Chip Registry
03

85% Direct Guild Share

We reject predatory wholesale margins. 85% of net proceeds go directly to the artisan workshop to fund raw materials, apprentice stipends, and the preservation of rare generational craft disciplines.

Bespoke Commissions
