THE ETHICS OF ACCUMULATION

The Four Pillars of the INK & CRAFT Handmade Charter

Every physical piece in our salon is governed by rigorous curatorial discipline. We reject industrial rapid prototyping, synthetic compounds, and anonymous supply chains.

01

Slow Materiality

Naturally harvested clay, organic fermentation vats, untreated heirloom timber, and botanical mordants. No synthetic binders or non-biodegradable polymers enter the artisan workflow.

CERTIFIED ECOLOGICAL
02

Physical NFC Ledger

A microscopic, tamper-proof ceramic NFC chip is physically embedded into each artifact during creation. Tapping your phone reveals the exact date, kiln batch, artisan signature, and permanent provenance ledger.

TAMPER-PROOF IDENTITY
03

Direct Guild Honorarium

85% of each acquisition is remitted directly to the independent maker and their apprenticeship guilds. This sustains generational master workshops that cannot compete with mechanized factories.

85% DIRECT RETURN
04

Bespoke Commissions

Patrons may initiate customized architectural commissions. Work directly with master joiners, glassblowers, and weavers with dedicated translation, progress log photobooks, and studio video visits.
