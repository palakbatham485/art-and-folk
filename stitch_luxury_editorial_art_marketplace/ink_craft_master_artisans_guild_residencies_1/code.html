Master in Focus • Living Treasure No. 04

Higashiyama Kiln Coordinates 35.0116° N, 135.7681° E • Kyoto, Japan
高橋
Anagama Kiln Wood-Fired Ceramics • 30-Year Practice 3 Bespoke Slots for Q1 2026

Mei Lin Takahashi

Master Ceramicist • Kyoto Pottery Guild, Higashiyama-ku

“The clay does not bend to our will; it enters an ancient, unspoken contract with mountain fire and pine ash over ninety-six uninterrupted hours.”

Takahashi fires her hillside Anagama kiln only twice per solar year, burning seasoned red pine at 1,320°C. Works remain unglazed; the vitreous patina, ochre blushes, and carbon flecks arise purely from flying wood ash melting upon the iron-dense local earth. Each vessel is certified with her vermilion seal and dual physical NFC ledger.

Institutional Manifesto

The INK & CRAFT Living Heritage Charter

Our alliance guarantees the financial and cultural sovereignty of independent atelier masters. We reject industrialized factory reproduction in favor of slow, accountable artistic continuity.

01 / 85%

Direct Creator Sovereign Royalty

Eighty-five percent of all primary acquisition proceeds transfer directly to the master’s workshop ledger within 72 hours of verification.

02 / 100%

Archival Material Purity Seal

Every pigment, clay vein, timber batch, and loom fibre is scientifically documented for mineral provenance and non-toxic permanence.

03 / NFC

Cryptographic Physical Ledger

Each masterwork embeds a tamper-evident microscopic NFC wafer registered to our decentralised living provenance archive.

04 / Guild

Apprenticeship Endowments

Ten percent of gallery commission margins fund multi-year stipends for next-generation apprentices at affiliated international ateliers.
